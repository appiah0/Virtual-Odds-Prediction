Good to know
Adding more picks to one slip lowers the chance that every pick wins. The slip card shows that chance so you can see it.

About Odds Reader

Odds Reader is a tool that reads an odds screenshot and turns the prices into probabilities. It removes the bookmaker's margin to show fair odds and points to the outcome the market rates as most likely.
What it cannot do. It cannot know the result of a virtual match. Those results come from a random number generator, so any pick can lose.

Questions

How are the picks made?

The app converts each odd to a probability, removes the margin, and ranks the outcomes. The top one is the pick.

Are results guaranteed?

No. Nothing is guaranteed and you can lose money.
